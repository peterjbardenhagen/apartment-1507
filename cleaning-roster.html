✅What to Clean — Each Fortnight

Complete all tasks below during your two-week duty. Spread across the fortnight — no need to do it all in one day. Tick off as you go.

🧹 Vacuuming & Floor Care

  • Vacuum hallway — full length, including edges and corners
  • Vacuum lounge room — under and around furniture, along baseboards
  • Vacuum rug — use proper suction, check for debris caught in weave
  • Vacuum kitchen — including under the dining table and island
  • Vacuum dining area — chairs moved, crumbs collected
  • Vacuum balcony — sweep loose dirt and plant debris
  • Robot vacuum: empty dust bag, check for blockages (run as needed)
  • Spot-mop timber floors with Earth Choice — barely damp mop only

🍳 Kitchen Deep Clean

  • Wipe down stove top — remove grates, clean burners, wipe surfaces
  • Clean oven — interior wiped, glass door cleaned, racks checked
  • Clean air fryer — basket and tray washed, exterior wiped
  • Clean microwave — interior wiped, turntable washed, exterior
  • Wipe kitchen benchtops — all surfaces including island with Glitz Stone Cleaner
  • Clean sink — scrub basin, polish tap, clear drain strainer
  • Wipe splashback and rangehood — degrease if needed
  • Clean toaster — empty crumb tray, wipe exterior
  • Wipe down cabinet fronts and handles
  • Take out kitchen bin — replace liner, spray with Glen 20
  • Return all items to their designated spots

🚿 Bathroom & Powder Room

  • Clean toilet — seat, lid, cistern, outside, flush button, base
  • Scrub toilet bowl with brush, clean brush holder
  • Clean vanity and basin — with appropriate cleaner
  • Clean mirror with Ajax Glass Cleaner
  • Wipe cupboard fronts, handles, light switches and door handles
  • Empty bathroom bin — replace liner, spray with Glen 20
  • Vacuum and mop bathroom floor — use Pine O Cleen, including behind toilet
  • Check floor drain for hair and dirt
  • Restock toilet paper — replace empty rolls
  • Restock liquid soap — top up dispenser

🛋️ Living & Common Areas

  • Dust all surfaces — shelves, TV unit, coffee table, window sills
  • Wipe dining table with Ajax Glass Cleaner
  • Fluff and arrange cushions on lounge
  • Fold or straighten blankets
  • Check and water plants — remove dead leaves
  • Tidy balcony — sweep, wipe surfaces, arrange furniture
  • Check balcony door threshold and tracks are clear of debris

🧽 Finishing Touches

  • Take out all rubbish and recycling — kitchen, bathroom, balcony
  • Wash dishcloth and tea towels — replace with clean ones
  • Replace sponge if worn
  • Check shopping list — flag any items running low
  • Report any maintenance issues (leaks, bulbs, broken items)
  • Do a final walk-through — "would I be happy coming home to this?"
⏱️ Estimated Time Full clean: approximately 1.5–2 hours. Break it up: vacuum one day, kitchen the next. The fortnightly schedule gives you flexibility.
💡 Daily Maintenance (Not on Roster — Everyone's Responsibility) Wipe benches after cooking. Wash dishes or load dishwasher after eating. Wipe the dining table after meals. Pick up hair after showering. Flush well and use the toilet brush if needed. If you spill it, clean it — don't wait for your roster week.

🤝During Your Off-Week

You're not on cleaning duty, but basic courtesy still applies:

  • Wipe benches after you cook
  • Rinse sink after brushing teeth or shaving
  • Flush toilet and use brush if needed
  • Pick up hair from shower/basin floor
  • Load and run dishwasher when full
  • Take out bin if it's full
  • Report breakages or issues promptly
🏆 The Golden Rule Leave shared spaces cleaner than you found them. A clean home is everyone's responsibility, every day — not just during your roster week.
