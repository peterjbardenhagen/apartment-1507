House Rules at a Glance

Core expectations for all residents, every day

🧹
Clean as you go. No food, crumbs, grease, dishes or rubbish left behind after cooking or eating.
🤝
Shared spaces are shared responsibility. Kitchen, powder room, balcony, laundry and living area.
🧴
Use the right product for each surface. Earth Choice for timber floors · Glitz for stone benches · Ajax for glass · Pine O Cleen for bathrooms.
💧
Keep water off timber flooring. Wipe rain, spills or mopping residue immediately.
🧺
Prefer the drying rack. Only use the dryer when needed. Always clean the lint filter after every load.
🫧

Dining Table & Placemats

Clean up after every meal

  1. Spray the table lightly with Ajax Spray & Wipe Glass Cleaner.
  2. Wipe with paper towel or a clean microfibre cloth.
  3. Remove all food crumbs, spills and marks.
  4. Pack placemats away in the cupboard.
  5. Do not leave plates, cups, food or rubbish on the table.
Supplies Ajax Glass Cleaner and paper towel are provided as part of the utilities contribution.

Balcony Door & Water Ingress

Important – prevents costly timber floor damage

⚠️ WarningThe side balcony door can allow rainwater to enter even when opened only slightly. Water can cross the black threshold directly onto the timber floorboards.
  • Only leave the side balcony door open 10–15 cm unless you are actively monitoring it.
  • Close the door immediately if rain, wind or storms are possible.
  • Never leave balcony doors open overnight or when leaving the apartment.
  • If rain water enters, close the door immediately and dry the black threshold and timber floor thoroughly.
  • Keep the door tracks and threshold clear of plant debris and leaves.
💡 Water-damaged timber flooring can stain, swell, cup or warp. Repairs are expensive and avoidable.

Cleaning the Lint Filter (Required After Every Load)

  1. Open the dryer door.
  2. Remove the lint filter from the bottom of the door opening.
  3. Peel off the lint and dispose of it in the bin.
  4. Reinstall the filter before the next use.
  5. Never run the dryer without the lint filter installed.

Microwave

Safe use and immediate clean-up

  • Use microwave-safe containers only — no metal, foil, cutlery or metallic trim.
  • Cover food to prevent splatter.
  • Remove food promptly after heating.
  • If food spills or splatters inside, wipe it immediately.
  • Do not spray cleaner directly inside the microwave — spray onto a cloth first.
💡 Typical costs: 2 min <1¢ · 5 min ~2–3¢ · 10 min ~5–7¢

Air Fryer – DeLonghi Ideal Fry

Clean after every use — especially greasy foods

After Normal Use

  1. Unplug and allow to cool completely.
  2. Remove food crumbs and residue.
  3. Wash removable components in warm soapy water.
  4. Dry thoroughly before reassembling.
  5. Wipe inside and outside with a damp cloth.

After Greasy Foods / Heavy Use

  1. Remove all removable parts.
  2. Wash with hot water and dishwashing liquid.
  3. Use a non-abrasive sponge for baked-on grease.
  4. Wipe basket edges, lid surrounds, heating element area and interior walls.
  5. Do not leave grease build-up — clean before the next person uses it.
💡 Running costs: 20 min ~$0.10–$0.15 · 40 min ~$0.20–$0.30

Toaster & Fridge

Safe use and surface care

Toaster

Settings: Browning dial 1–2 light, 3–4 medium, 5–6 dark. Use Cancel to stop early, Reheat for already-toasted bread, Defrost for frozen bread.

Safety: Never insert metal objects. Unplug before cleaning.

Cleaning the Toaster

  1. Switch off at the wall and unplug.
  2. Allow to cool fully.
  3. Remove and empty the crumb tray into the bin.
  4. Shake loose crumbs into the bin.
  5. Wipe the outside and wipe down the bench.

Fridge – Stainless Steel Care

Current stateThe fridge currently has fingerprints, streaks and water marks. It needs a proper clean with stainless steel cleaner (add to shopping list).
  • Use stainless steel cleaner or stainless steel wipes (to purchase — see shopping list).
  • Spray product onto a cloth — not directly onto the fridge.
  • Wipe with the grain of the steel, not against it.
  • Buff dry with a clean microfibre cloth.
  • Do not use steel wool, bleach, abrasive pads or harsh chemicals.

Floor Cleaning Guidelines

Timber and bathroom floors

Herringbone Timber Flooring

  • Vacuum or use the robot vacuum regularly.
  • Mop with Earth Choice Floor & Surface diluted with water — use a barely damp mop only.
  • Never leave standing water on timber.
  • No steam mop, bleach, harsh degreasers or excessive water.
  • Dry any wet patches immediately.

Bathroom Floors

  • Use diluted Pine O Cleen for disinfecting.
  • Mop around the toilet base, vanity and corners.
  • Allow to dry fully before use.
🧽

Cleaning Cloths & Tea Towels

Which cloth to use where, and how to care for them

  • Blue cleaning cloths: benches, appliances and general wipe-downs.
  • Black microfibre cloths: drying, polishing, glass and stainless steel.
  • Never leave damp cloths screwed up on benches or in the sink.
  • Wash greasy cloths promptly — warm wash 40–60°C, regular detergent, no fabric softener.
  • Ensure cloths are fully dry before returning to the cupboard.

Powder Room / Guest Bathroom

Shared responsibility — after each use and weekly

After Every Use

  • Rinse toothpaste, soap residue and hair from the basin.
  • Check the toilet bowl and use the brush if needed.
  • Hang towels neatly — never leave towels on the floor.
  • Place rubbish in the bin.

Weekly Clean (15–20 minutes)

  1. Clear all surfaces.
  2. Clean the vanity and basin with an appropriate cleaner.
  3. Clean the mirror with glass cleaner.
  4. Clean the toilet seat, lid, cistern, outside bowl, flush button and base.
  5. Scrub the bowl with the toilet brush.
  6. Clean and disinfect the toilet brush holder.
  7. Empty the bin, replace the liner, spray with Glen 20 if needed.
  8. Vacuum then mop the floor, including behind and around the toilet.
  9. Check the floor drain for hair and dirt.
  10. Wipe cupboard fronts, handles, light switches and door handles.

Balcony Cleaning & Plants

Shared responsibility for cleanliness and plant care

NoteDo not scrub or pressure-clean the current balcony tiles — they are being replaced. Keep the balcony neat and free of loose dirt and debris.

Current Tiles – Maintenance

  • Sweep plant debris, leaves, soil and dirt regularly.
  • Use a dustpan and broom — do not leave swept-up dirt in piles.
  • Place plant debris into a garbage bag or waste box and empty regularly.
  • Do not bring wet or leaking plant waste through the apartment — drain it first.
  • Do not sweep debris over the balcony edge.

Future White Porcelain Tiles

  • Sweep weekly.
  • Spot-clean soil, fertiliser and pot marks quickly.
  • Mop monthly with warm water and a mild cleaner.
  • Always lift heavy pots — never drag them across the tiles.
  • Keep pot trays clean and check for water staining.

Plant Watering Guide

Most balcony plants are succulents, cacti or drought-tolerant varieties — avoid overwatering.

SeasonFrequency (if no rain)

Door Closer Repair – Peter to Fix

The existing hardware appears to be a failed push-to-open latch and magnetic catch.

What to Buy (Bunnings / Mitre 10)

  • 2× white push-to-open cabinet latches (Hafele / Kaboodle / Hettich style)
  • 2× white magnetic cabinet catches with metal strike plates
  • Small screws (if not included)

Search for: push open cabinet latch · push to open cupboard latch · magnetic cabinet catch · Hafele push latch

Installation Steps

  1. Remove the broken latch and magnetic catch.
  2. Use existing screw holes where possible.
  3. Install the new push latch in the same position.
  4. Close the door gently and mark where the plunger meets the door frame.
  5. Install the strike plate at that contact point.
  6. Adjust until the door closes flush and pops open when pushed.
