Miele Oven – Splatters and Residue

Built-in electric oven — clean after heavy use

The Miele oven interior showed grease splatters, minor food residue on the side walls and floor, and accumulated dust around the back fan vent and light fixture. Racks had minor discolouration from use.

⚠️ Required Action Wipe down the oven interior after use — especially after cooking fatty or splattering foods. Do not leave baked-on residue for the next person.

How to Clean the Miele Oven

  1. Let the oven cool completely before attempting to clean.
  2. Remove racks and side supports — pull out wire racks. Soak in warm soapy water if heavily soiled.
  3. Wipe loose debris — use a damp microfibre cloth to remove crumbs and loose residue from the floor and walls.
  4. Apply oven cleaner — spray oven cleaner onto a cloth or sponge (not directly onto the fan). Apply to interior walls, floor and ceiling. Avoid the rear fan vent and light fixture.
  5. Let it sit per product instructions (typically 5–10 minutes). Do not use oven cleaner on the glass door — use a mild degreaser or baking soda paste instead.
  6. Wipe clean — use a damp cloth to remove all cleaner residue. Repeat with fresh water until no residue remains.
  7. Clean the racks — scrub with a non-abrasive sponge or scourer, rinse and dry before sliding back into the oven.
  8. Wipe the door glass — use a glass cleaner or a baking soda paste on stubborn baked-on splatters. Buff dry with a clean cloth.
  9. Wipe the exterior — stainless steel surfaces with stainless steel cleaner or a damp microfibre cloth, following the grain. Do not use steel wool or abrasive pads.
⚠️ Miele-Specific Notes
  • Do not spray cleaner directly onto the rear fan — spray onto a cloth first.
  • Do not use steam cleaners inside the oven — can damage electronics.
  • Do not use abrasive scouring pads on the interior enamel or glass door.
  • If the oven has a pyrolytic self-clean cycle, refer to the manual before using — it runs at very high temperatures.
  • Wipe the door seal/gasket gently — it can tear if scrubbed aggressively.

Air Fryer – Grease Build-Up

DeLonghi Ideal Fry – requires cleaning after every use

The air fryer was found with accumulated grease and food residue on the basket, interior walls and surrounding components. This build-up indicates it was used multiple times without being cleaned between or after use.

⚠️ Required Action The air fryer must be cleaned after every use — including after cooking greasy foods. Do not leave it for the next person.

How to Clean It (After Greasy or Heavy Use)

  1. Unplug and allow to cool fully before touching any components.
  2. Remove all removable parts — basket, tray and any inserts.
  3. Wash with hot water and dishwashing liquid using a non-abrasive sponge.
  4. Clean the basket edges, lid surround, heating element area and interior walls.
  5. Dry all parts thoroughly before reassembling and storing.

Main Bathroom Toilet – Persistent Smell

Toilet odour noticeable on entering the apartment since the party

There is a persistent toilet smell on entering the apartment. The powder room toilet has been cleaned but the smell remains — it is coming from the main bathroom toilet.

⚠️ Not Acceptable A recurring toilet smell in the common area is not acceptable. This must be resolved with a thorough deep clean immediately.

Evidence

The following issues were observed on inspection (Stylus brand toilet, dual flush 4.5L/3.0L):

  • Toilet bowl contains discoloured water that has not been flushed — indicates the toilet was used and left.
  • Visible grime and dark specks on the bowl rim and interior surfaces above the water line.
  • Dirt and dust accumulated around the hinge mechanism — seat/lid attachment points have yellowish grime build-up.
  • Dust and smudges on the flush button and cistern top.
  • Dirt and debris on the floor around the toilet base, including in the wall-floor junction.

Deep Clean — Step by Step

This is a full deep clean, not a quick wipe. Expect 20–30 minutes.

  1. Clear the area. Remove everything from the cistern top. Move the bin, brush holder and any items from around the toilet base.
  2. Apply toilet cleaner. Squirt a thick ring of toilet cleaner (Domestos or similar bleach-based) around the inside of the bowl, under the rim. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes. Do not flush.
  3. Clean flush button and cistern top. Wipe the flush button and entire cistern top with Pine O Cleen spray on a microfibre cloth. Pay attention to the crevice around the button.
  4. Remove seat and lid. Stylus toilets typically have quick-release hinges — press the release buttons on the hinges and lift the seat/lid assembly off the bowl. If unable to remove, hinge areas must be cleaned with a small brush.
  5. Clean seat, lid and hinges. Scrub the underside of the seat and lid, all hinge components and the hinge mounting points on the bowl. Use a small brush or old toothbrush to get into crevices. Wipe down with Pine O Cleen.
  6. Scrub the bowl. Use the toilet brush to scrub the entire interior — under the rim, around the water line, and deep into the trap. Flush. Repeat if water is still discoloured.
  7. Clean the exterior. Wipe the entire exterior of the bowl — cistern, sides, front, back, and the pedestal base. Use Pine O Cleen on the ceramic and a damp cloth for general dust.
  8. Disinfect the brush holder. Pour a small amount of bleach or toilet cleaner into the brush holder, swirl, and let sit for 5 minutes. Rinse thoroughly.
  9. Mop the floor. Move the toilet brush holder and bin. Vacuum then mop the floor around and behind the toilet with Pine O Cleen.
  10. Reassemble. Reattach the seat and lid. Return the bin and brush holder. Restock toilet paper.
🔁 Daily Maintenance — Every Use
  • Flush the toilet after every use. Do not leave the bowl unflushed.
  • Use the toilet brush if there are any marks in the bowl — brush, then flush.
  • Close the lid before flushing to prevent aerosol spray.
  • Check the floor for splashes or drips — wipe immediately.
💡 Preventing Toilet Odours Toilet smells are usually caused by:
• Urine or waste left in the bowl — flush after every use.
• Dirt trapped under the seat rim or around hinges — lift the seat and clean the hinge area weekly.
• Splashes on the floor, pedestal or wall — check and wipe after every use.
• The toilet brush not being rinsed — rinse the brush after use and let it drip dry into the holder.
