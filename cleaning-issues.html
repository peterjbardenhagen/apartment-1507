What Was Found – Overview

A summary of the condition of shared spaces last weekend

The shared areas of the apartment were found in an unacceptable state last weekend. The kitchen, sink, cookware, floor, air fryer, balcony, powder room and shared cleaning items all showed clear signs of having been left dirty after use.

This is a shared apartment. Every resident is responsible for leaving shared spaces clean — not just passable — after each use. Cleaning cannot be deferred until it accumulates into a large job, and it is not fair or reasonable for one person to repeatedly clean up avoidable mess left by others.

⚠️ Core Expectation Every shared space must be returned to the same or better condition than it was found in. This is non-negotiable.

Air Fryer – Grease Build-Up

DeLonghi Ideal Fry – requires cleaning after every use

The air fryer was found with accumulated grease and food residue on the basket, interior walls and surrounding components. This build-up indicates it was used multiple times without being cleaned between or after use.

⚠️ Required Action The air fryer must be cleaned after every use — including after cooking greasy foods. Do not leave it for the next person.

How to Clean It (After Greasy or Heavy Use)

  1. Unplug and allow to cool fully before touching any components.
  2. Remove all removable parts — basket, tray and any inserts.
  3. Wash with hot water and dishwashing liquid using a non-abrasive sponge.
  4. Clean the basket edges, lid surround, heating element area and interior walls.
  5. Dry all parts thoroughly before reassembling and storing.

Powder Room – Neglected Condition

Shared bathroom – shared responsibility

The powder room was found in a poor state. As a shared bathroom used by all residents and any visitors, it should be cleaned lightly after each use and thoroughly on a weekly basis.

⚠️ Not Acceptable The powder room should never be allowed to reach this level of neglect. It is a shared responsibility — not one person's job.

After Every Single Use

  • Rinse toothpaste, soap residue and hair from the basin immediately.
  • Check the toilet bowl — use the brush if needed before leaving.
  • Hang towels neatly on the rail — never leave them on the floor.
  • Place any rubbish in the bin.

Weekly Clean Required

  1. Clear all surfaces.
  2. Clean vanity and basin with appropriate cleaner.
  3. Wipe the mirror with glass cleaner.
  4. Clean the toilet — seat, lid, cistern, exterior bowl, flush button and base.
  5. Scrub the bowl with the toilet brush.
  6. Disinfect the toilet brush holder.
  7. Empty the bin, replace the liner, spray with Glen 20 if needed.
  8. Vacuum then mop the floor including around and behind the toilet.
  9. Check the floor drain for hair and dirt.
  10. Wipe light switches, door handle and cupboard fronts.

Balcony – Plant Debris and Neglect

Balcony cleanliness is a shared responsibility

The balcony had accumulated plant debris, soil and loose dirt that had not been swept or cleared. The door track area also showed built-up grime.

Note Do not scrub or pressure-clean the existing tiles — they are scheduled for replacement. The immediate need is regular sweeping and keeping the area free of loose debris.

Required – Ongoing Maintenance

  • Sweep plant debris, leaves, soil and dirt regularly using a dustpan and broom.
  • Place all swept debris into a garbage bag — do not leave it in piles.
  • Do not sweep debris over the balcony edge.
  • Do not bring wet or leaking plant waste through the apartment — drain pots over the balcony first.
  • Keep the balcony door tracks and black threshold free of grime and leaves.

Fridge – Fingerprints and Streaks

Stainless steel exterior needs a proper clean

The stainless steel fridge exterior has visible fingerprints, water marks and streaks. Stainless steel cleaner or wipes are needed — these are currently on the shopping list to purchase.

To Do Purchase stainless steel cleaner or wipes. Spray onto a cloth (not directly onto the fridge), wipe with the grain, and buff dry with a clean microfibre cloth. Do not use abrasive pads, steel wool or bleach.

The Required Standard – Going Forward

This document has been written to make the expectations clear. The apartment is a shared home and everyone has an equal responsibility to maintain it.

  • Clean up immediately after cooking, eating, washing up or using an appliance.
  • Do not leave food scraps, dirty cutlery, greasy pans, damp cloths or rubbish for someone else to deal with.
  • Use the correct cleaning product for each surface — see the instruction manual.
  • If something spills, leaks, stains or gets greasy, clean it immediately.
  • Shared spaces — kitchen, powder room, balcony, laundry — are everyone's responsibility equally.
  • Return every item to its correct location after use.
Expected standard: clean · dry · tidy · presentable · ready for the next person.
